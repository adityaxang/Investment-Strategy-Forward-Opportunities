Exercise Brief

The task: select one Asia-focused PE firm from a list of ten, then produce a 3-slide analysis (max) demonstrating market understanding, analytical reasoning, and commercial acumen, followed by a 10-minute live presentation. Firm chosen: Warburg Pincus Asia.

Slide Structure

1. Firm Spotlight

Summary — $85B+ global AUM, $10B+ deployed in India across 80+ investments, $30M–$1B cheque size, growth equity + control buyouts
Investment thesis — targets scaled platforms for next-phase growth via strategic partnership; value creation through operational efficiency, geographic expansion, margin expansion, and strategic M&A
Portfolio logic — separates what the firm publicly states, what its deal pattern reveals (stage diversification, FS value-chain investments), and what can be inferred (platform expansion through vertical depth)

2. IDFC FIRST Deal Deep Dive

Deal snapshot — ₹4,876 Cr (~$587M) primary capital raise via CCPS for a 9.99% stake at ₹60/share; announced April 2025, RBI-approved July 2025
Rationale — geographic clustering (75% of FS capital in India), sub-sector sequencing (Vistaar → TruHome → IDFC FIRST as the "anchor"), and a 10-year relationship with the CEO
Key risks & mitigations — profitability pressure from the MFI crisis, governance friction at the EGM, asset quality risk, and post-raise capital requirements, each paired with an observed mitigant (e.g. CAR strengthening from 16.1% to 18.9%)

3. Forward Sourcing

Two priority themes: deploying WPFS III capital ($3B fund) into the financial services value chain, and mid-market, export-oriented "under the radar" businesses with import-substitution potential
Pine Labs — payments/merchant commerce, sized to WPFS III's $300–400M ticket range; principal risk is UPI cannibalisation of PoS MDR revenue, mitigated by its BNPL/credit pivot
Sahajanand Medical Tech (SMT) — minimally invasive cardiovascular devices, India-manufactured with global export potential; principal risk is FDA approval delays, mitigated by prioritising CE Mark markets (Middle East, SEA/LatAm)
Sourcing

All claims are drawn from and cited to: Warburg Pincus press releases, IDFC FIRST Bank's BSE filings and investor presentations, RBI approval filings and banking statistics, CEO earnings calls, EGM voting results, Forrester Research, Redseer, TechCrunch, and company RHP/DRHP filings. Each slide distinguishes publicly stated information from analyst inference.
